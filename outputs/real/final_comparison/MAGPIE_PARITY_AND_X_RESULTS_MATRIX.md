## Circularity Boundary

- Modes A-B exclude prior pathogenicity predictors and are the low-circularity MAGPIE-parity comparison.
- Modes C-G intentionally include 86 dbNSFP prior-predictor fields. Modes E-G also use AlphaMissense; modes B, D, F, and G use SpliceAI.
- Mode G is two-layer GraphSAGE residual logit correction, not feature stacking. Its loss uses training labels only; test labels are hidden until final evaluation.
- None of the controlled A-G rows uses SwissProt/HUMSAVAR for training. That resource remains external validation.
- No row is full-490k, gene-held-out, temporal, or final external validation evidence.
